4. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device; local browser storage and tracking pixels work similarly. We use them in accordance with the Polish Electronic Communications Law and the GDPR.

You give your consent in the banner that appears on your first visit. The “Accept all” and “Reject” buttons carry equal weight, and under “Settings” you can choose individual categories. Not consenting does not limit your use of the website or booking. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via the link in the website footer. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before its withdrawal. We store your choice (categories and date) in your browser’s local storage — it is needed to demonstrate consent and does not require separate consent.

5. Your rights

You have the right to: access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict processing, data portability and object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Where we process data on the basis of consent, you may withdraw it at any time — without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, write to the email address given in section 1. We will reply without undue delay, at the latest within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland, uodo.gov.pl.

6. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk: an encrypted HTTPS connection, restricted access to data and a secured administration panel.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, e.g. when the law or the way the website works changes. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update.

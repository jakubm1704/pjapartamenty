Tours for our guests

Besides a comfortable apartment, we can arrange a trip beyond the walls of the Old Town — from the salt mine to the Tatra Mountains. This is handled by our partner, Kraków Direct.

Wieliczka Salt Mine

Underground chambers, lakes and chapels carved in salt — one of the most visited attractions in Poland, a short drive from Kraków.

Auschwitz-Birkenau

A guided visit to the Memorial site — for guests who want to learn the history of this place.

Zakopane and the Tatras

A full-day trip to the mountains — highland architecture, oscypek cheese and views of the Tatras.

Old Town with a guide

A walking tour of the Main Market Square, Wawel and Kazimierz — without leaving the centre.

Dunajec river rafting

A traditional raft trip through the Dunajec Gorge — a scenic route in the Pieniny Mountains.

A tour tailored to you

Have something specific in mind? Write to us and we will adapt a tour to your time and interests.

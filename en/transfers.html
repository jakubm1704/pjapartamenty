Airport transfers

We will pick you up from Kraków-Balice or Katowice-Pyrzowice airport and take you straight to your apartment door — no searching for a taxi after a long flight. The same comfort on the way back.

Airport transfer ↔ Kraków city centre — price per one-way trip
Passengers Vehicle Kraków-Balice Airport Katowice-Pyrzowice Airport
1–4 passengers Car €35 (150 PLN) €150 (600 PLN)
5–6 passengers Van €50 (200 PLN) €200 (800 PLN)
7–8 passengers Larger van / Sprinter €70 (300 PLN) €300 (1,200 PLN)
9+ passengers Minibus / Sprinter Price on request

Prices in euro are shown with the PLN equivalent. The price is for a one-way trip and depends only on the number of passengers — not on the amount of luggage. You book the return transfer in the same way, as a separate trip. The price includes airport pickup and a direct drive to your apartment address. For groups of 9+ passengers the price is agreed individually — please contact us.

How it works

1. Booking

Send us your flight number and arrival time — ideally when you book your apartment, or via the contact form.

2. Pickup

The driver will contact you before your flight to arrange the pickup details, then take you directly to your apartment.

3. Check-in

Straight to your apartment address — no transfers and no hunting for a bus stop.
